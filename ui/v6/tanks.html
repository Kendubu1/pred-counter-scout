How this is counted

  • Soaked per death is damage mitigated plus damage taken from heroes, divided by deaths, over all of that group's games.
  • Winning means the win rate is 50% or better after pulling it 100 games toward 50%, so a small lucky sample cannot qualify. A build that soaks the most and loses is left off.
  • Each row is one hero in one lane on one Eternal, with 150 or more ranked games of ten minutes or longer.
  • Rank is the rank the player held in that game. Silver+ means Silver and every rank above it, and the same for Gold+ and Plat+. Silver+ needs 100 games per row, Gold+ 60 and Plat+ 30, because those groups are smaller. Plat+ rows are thin, so check the games under "More". Unplaced players count under All ranks only.
  • The augment, crest and items shown are what those players run most. They describe the build; they are not ranked picks.
  • Which Eternal compares an Eternal with the same hero in the same lane on every other Eternal, over the lane's tank heroes (300+ games, soaking at least 1.2 times the lane average). It says "wins more" or "wins less" only when the whole margin is on one side of zero.
  • Teams that are ahead die less, so part of each win rate is "winning made them hard to kill", not only the other way round.
