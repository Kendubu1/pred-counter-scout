How this is counted
  • A row is one hero in one lane on one Eternal: the Eternal that hero does the job best on, among those that win. Its other winning Eternals are under "More".
  • Winning means the win rate is 50% or better after pulling it 100 games toward 50%, so a small lucky sample cannot qualify. A build that leads the job and loses is left off.
  • Games are ranked games of ten minutes or longer. A row needs 150 games at All ranks, 100 at Silver+, 60 at Gold+ and 30 at Plat+. Plat+ rows are thin.
  • Rank is the rank the player held in that game, that rank and above. Unplaced players count under All ranks only.
  • Shielding is the shields a support's four teammates received. The match data does not record who gave a shield.
  • Best Eternal compares an Eternal with the same hero in the same lane on every other Eternal, over the heroes that are good at the job. Green and red show only when the whole margin is on one side of zero.
  • The augment, crest and items on a row are what those players run most. They describe the build; they are not ranked picks.
  • Teams that are ahead die less and deal more, so part of every win rate here is winning producing the numbers, not only the other way round.
