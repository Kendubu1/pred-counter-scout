Patch 1.18 is live: Kaelen's page, the new items, fresh dataNov 3

Across the site

  • Hero and item numbers are 1.18. Every ability and item change from the patch is in, including Kaelen, Vortex Stone and Forticus with their real icons.
  • 1.18 match data starts today. Win rates, builds, augments and boards now count 1.18 games only, so for the first few days they rest on small samples. A note under the patch bar says how many ranked matches are in so far, and links to the 1.17 boards until 1.18 fills in.
  • Kaelen has a hero page. His builds and picks fill in as his games come in.

Game reviews: everyone's rank, and the enemy team's buildsNov 3

  • Ranks after the game. Each game review's summary now lists your whole team, randoms included, with the rank and VP they finished the game on and how much VP they gained or lost. A green arrow marks a rank-up.
  • The enemy team, for matchup reference. Open "Evidence & replay details" to see each enemy player's rank and VP, score, the lane they played against, their finished items and the Eternal, minors, augment and crest they ran. Tap any icon for the full description.
  • The finishing rank is worked out from the VP number, so it doesn't show any demotion protection the game may give.

Patch 1.18 Frostbite review: predictions, ranked comps and the new heroNov 2

Live draft counter

  • Counters now come from real games. Each suggested pick shows its actual lane win rate against that enemy and how many games it's based on (ranked, this patch, 20 or more games). Before, the list came from our kit-math simulator, which is still unproven. The green and red fight dots are gone with it.
  • Small samples can't top the list. Each counter's win rate is now adjusted for how many games it's based on: a small record is pulled toward what the two heroes' overall lane win rates predict, so 15 wins in 24 games no longer beats a solid record over 800 games. The as-played rate shows under it when the two differ. The draft page also only uses a record when it mostly comes from the lane you're countering. The same ranking now runs on the counter list on each hero's page.
  • Drag heroes into lanes. A hero list now sits beside the lanes. Drag a hero onto any Enemy or You slot, or tap a hero and then tap the slot. This works on phones too. You can also drag a placed hero to another lane to swap them, which fixes a wrong lane guess (like a mid Argus landing in support).
  • Add our pick. A new button adds your team's picks the same way as enemy picks, every pick of yours has its own move button, and tapping your hero's name opens their build.

Patch review

  • Every 1.18 change, with our call on each. Twelve heroes (new hero Kaelen included), seven Eternals and eleven items, each with what the change does and what it means for your ranked games, next to where that hero stood on 1.17.
  • Ranked comps. A new section on how team compositions shift: frontliners and the two new shield items, the haste and gold cuts on mages, Krix on jungle bruisers, and the duo lane.
  • The ranked reset. Split 5 ends, everyone re-places, Master arrives between Diamond and Paragon, and Paragon becomes the top 100.
  • Predicted builds for Kaelen. His card on the 1.18 review now shows two example builds with item icons: an offlane frontliner (Overlord, Rapture, Forticus) and a jungle attack-speed build with Relentless Hunt (Sky Splitter, Ashbringer, Onixian Quiver), each with an Eternal and an augment. These are our guesses from his kit and from what similar heroes build, since nobody has played him yet. Measured builds replace them once 1.18 ranked games come in.
  • What it means for you. Each squad member's report now opens with a "What patch 1.18 means for you" card: which of your heroes changed and our call on each (with the lane you play them in), the Eternals, augments and items you actually run that changed, Kaelen if jungle or offlane is one of your main lanes, and the ranked reset from your rank. The Player reports page shows the one-line summary on every card.
  • These are predictions. Measured win rates replace them once enough 1.18 ranked games come in. Kaelen gets his hero page once the game data publishes him after release.

Rank filter on phones

  • One filter bar for rank and patch. The homepage has a single bar for rank and patch, with the number of matches behind what you're looking at. It drives the meta board, the full lane pages and Most banned together, so you can flip between ranks without hunting for buttons. On a computer it sits under the lane buttons and sticks to the top of the screen as you scroll; on a phone it's pinned to the bottom (and steps aside while you type in the hero search).
  • Look back at patch 1.16. The patch picker (bottom bar on phones, next to the rank buttons on a computer) switches the meta board, Most banned and the lane pages to the final 1.16 numbers: 233,348 ranked matches, split by rank the same way. Hero pages and builds stay on the current patch. When 1.18 data arrives, 1.17 joins the list.

Lane rooms

  • Every win rate follows your rank and patch. The "rest of the lane" and "also flexed into" heroes used to show all-ranks numbers from the current patch whatever you picked. They now change with the rank and patch buttons, like the ranked picks above them. A hero with under 30 games at that rank and patch shows "few games" instead of a win rate.
  • Counter records say what they cover. A note above the "Countering their pick" list says which rank and patch its head-to-head records come from, and flags it when that isn't what you have selected.
  • Counters by rank, from patch 1.18. Starting with 1.18, head-to-head records are also split by rank, so picking Diamond+ shows how a matchup goes in Diamond+ games. Each patch's counters are also kept when the next patch arrives, so the patch buttons can show them later. 1.17 and earlier stay all ranks.
  • Easier to read on a computer. Each hero's win rate now sits next to its name instead of at the far side of the screen. On a wide screen the ranked picks sit on the left and the rest of the lane and the top players sit beside them on the right.
  • The right hero next to each top player. The hero shown next to a top-ranked player is now the one they play most in that lane. Before, it was their most-played hero overall, so a carry player who mostly plays mid showed a mid hero (Purpleswan showed Neon instead of Twinblast).

A tidier menu

  • Meta comes first: Pick & Build, Most banned and the patch review.
  • Coach holds Game review, Player reports and the Ban planner.
  • Ban planner is its own page now, under Coach: who our squad should ban and who stomps us. Most banned keeps the ladder-wide boards.
  • The Climb is now called How to play ranked.
  • Live draft counter is back under Experimental. Its counters came from our kit-math sim, which is still unproven, so treat them as theory. (Since Nov 2 they come from real lane records.)

See who queued in 2- and 3-stack games, and which lanes to try nextOct 31

Game reviews

  • Who queued. Every 2-, 3- or 4-stack game review now opens with a card listing the squad members in it: their hero, lane, kills/deaths/assists, their career win rate in that lane, and whether this game was above or under the norm for that hero in that lane.
  • Lanes to queue after a loss. When you lose and the lanes you played aren't your best seats as a group (2+ more wins per 100 games available), or someone had a rough game for their lane, the card suggests the lanes to queue next time, scored the same way as the 5-stack picker, including how your carry + support pair does laning together.
  • A rough game in your best lane keeps the lane. If someone struggled but was already in their best seat, the card says to keep the lane instead of moving them.

Picks that say how sure they are, a review on every coach pageOct 27

Hero pages

  • Every augment, Eternal and crest pick now says how sure it is. Best means it clearly wins more. Leans means it's ahead but could flip. Toss-up means the top options win about the same: the pick with the best odds still leads, the others it's tied with are marked Even, and that list opens by itself so you can see when to take them.
  • A small sample can't take the top spot. A pick's win rate counts for more the more games it has, and a pick needs a fair share of the lane's games to lead.
  • Plat+ picks are more useful on thin lanes. When Plat+ only has a few dozen games, each pick leans on how it does at all ranks, so a pick that's winning in Plat+ (like Aion on Argus support) can lead instead of whatever is most played.
  • See how popular each pick is: every row shows its share of players in that lane, like "18% of junglers".
  • Eternal lines explain the fit. Each Eternal says how it works with that hero's abilities and play style, for every Eternal shown, including the Plat+ ones.

Coach

  • New Reports page, its own tab next to Coach. It opens with how we play together: the best lineup when all five queue (with heroes), the best pairs and trios, bot-lane pairings, and the team habit to fix. Below that, a card for each player with their rank, top strength and top thing to work on, which opens their full review.
  • Clearer lineup. Switch between the Best, Your usual and Worst lineup, each with one rating and a short reason. Heroes only show from 20+ of your own games in that role, with the count on every hero.
  • Since the patch. The lineup opens on your games since the latest patch, so the heroes you have been playing lately show up (5+ games, like Goldilocks324's Wukong jungle). Tap All-time for the career view.
  • Your review now sits near the top of each squad member's coach page: what to keep doing and what to work on, each with the numbers behind it and a plain next step.
  • One page, less repetition. The ledger, the film room and the plan are folded into the review: the wins-per-100 numbers now sit on the matching tip, recent form sits under the road to Platinum, and the pentakill receipts are at the bottom of the review.
  • Everyone is held to the same rules. A tip only shows when the numbers clear a set bar, so some pages have more tips than others.
  • Tips compare you three ways: with your own lanes and heroes, with the rest of the squad in the games you played together, and with the other players in your role in those same ranked games (wards, deaths, damage, gold and kill share).
  • Each tip says how sure it is. Pattern, not proof marks a link in the games that may have another cause.
  • Fairer lane and fight tips. A lane you still win in is now called a backup, not a lane to avoid. The fights tip compares you with how the squad's fights usually go, since every one of you sees the team win fewer of the fights you are in.
  • Recent form counts. Lane numbers are all-time, so the review now also checks your last 30 days of squad games. A lane you have been winning lately (like a 9-2 Wukong jungle run) is called out as on form and is never the lane you are told to avoid.

Augment popups, more items per buildOct 26

Hero pages

  • Simulator picks are off for now. Counters, builds, augments and Eternals show only what ranked players actually win with. Counter picks without enough head-to-head games sit at the bottom, unranked. The simulator comes back once it's polished.
  • Tap any augment to see what it does, why to take it, and every other augment for that hero side by side: what each one does, when to take it, and its pick and win rate in your lane, plus the augment's numbers in the hero's other lanes.
  • Beyond the core under each build: the other items players commonly build on that hero, split into core swaps, items 4–6 and situational picks, each with a reason. Items already in the open build are left out.
  • Augment advice rewritten for this week's games. It now always agrees with the Best augment on the page; every other augment says when it is worth taking instead.
  • Every Eternal popup looks the same: the most-picked minors are marked, and who runs it sits at the bottom. Beyond the core is trimmed to the items, with a green +3 wins per 100 games on the ones that win more than a typical item for that hero.
  • Builds say which ranks they show. Plat+ builds need one three-item set with 20 Plat+ games, so most lanes still show all-rank builds under Plat+ for now. The Builds heading now says so.
  • All augments, Eternals and crests are back under Plat+. Plat+ lanes hold only a few hundred games, so some picks were dropping off the list. Every augment shows again, with up to three Eternals and three crests. Picks with too few games to rank are marked few games and can't be Best.

Squad coach

  • Rotations get credit. The coach now knows which lane every kill and tower was in, so a mid who leaves lane to avenge a teammate gets named for it. Staying in your lane while its towers still stand counts as the right play. Once your lane's enemy tower is down, the coach expects you to rotate.
  • No more "never joined" for fights that weren't one fight. Two picks on opposite sides of the map a few seconds apart are now read as two plays, so nobody gets blamed for missing both.
  • Reviews lead with what decided the game. The headline names the cause, then a short ranked list of what lost it (or won it), then one lesson for next game. The times and who-died-where behind each point sit underneath. Newer reviews use it first.
  • Shorter build & matchup breakdowns. Under Evidence & replay details, each section (build decision, lane threats, items, Eternal, augment, crest) is now a short list of points instead of a paragraph.
