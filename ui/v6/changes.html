What's new

What changed on Pred Scout, newest first. What's coming is at the bottom.

Picks that say how sure they are, a review on every coach pageOct 27

Hero pages

  • Every augment, Eternal and crest pick now says how sure it is. Best means it clearly wins more. Leans means it's ahead but could flip. Toss-up means the top options win about the same: the pick with the best odds still leads, the others it's tied with are marked Even, and that list opens by itself so you can see when to take them.
  • A small sample can't take the top spot. A pick's win rate counts for more the more games it has, and a pick needs a fair share of the lane's games to lead.
  • Plat+ picks are more useful on thin lanes. When Plat+ only has a few dozen games, each pick leans on how it does at all ranks, so a pick that's winning in Plat+ (like Aion on Argus support) can lead instead of whatever is most played.
  • See how popular each pick is: every row shows its share of players in that lane, like "18% of junglers".
  • Eternal lines explain the fit. Each Eternal says how it works with that hero's abilities and play style, for every Eternal shown, including the Plat+ ones.

Coach

  • New Reports page, its own tab next to Coach. It opens with how we play together: the best lineup when all five queue (with heroes), the best pairs and trios, bot-lane pairings, and the team habit to fix. Below that, a card for each player with their rank, top strength and top thing to work on, which opens their full review.
  • Your review now sits near the top of each squad member's coach page: what to keep doing and what to work on, each with the numbers behind it and a plain next step.
  • One page, less repetition. The ledger, the film room and the plan are folded into the review: the wins-per-100 numbers now sit on the matching tip, recent form sits under the road to Platinum, and the pentakill receipts are at the bottom of the review.
  • Everyone is held to the same rules. A tip only shows when the numbers clear a set bar, so some pages have more tips than others.
  • Tips compare you three ways: with your own lanes and heroes, with the rest of the squad in the games you played together, and with the other players in your role in those same ranked games (wards, deaths, damage, gold and kill share).
  • Each tip says how sure it is. Pattern, not proof marks a link in the games that may have another cause.
  • Fairer lane and fight tips. A lane you still win in is now called a backup, not a lane to avoid. The fights tip compares you with how the squad's fights usually go, since every one of you sees the team win fewer of the fights you are in.
  • Recent form counts. Lane numbers are all-time, so the review now also checks your last 30 days of squad games. A lane you have been winning lately (like a 9-2 Wukong jungle run) is called out as on form and is never the lane you are told to avoid.

Augment popups, more items per buildOct 26

Hero pages

  • Simulator picks are off for now. Counters, builds, augments and Eternals show only what ranked players actually win with. Counter picks without enough head-to-head games sit at the bottom, unranked. The simulator comes back once it's polished.
  • Tap any augment to see what it does, why to take it, and every other augment for that hero side by side: what each one does, when to take it, and its pick and win rate in your lane, plus the augment's numbers in the hero's other lanes.
  • Beyond the core under each build: the other items players commonly build on that hero, split into core swaps, items 4–6 and situational picks, each with a reason. Items already in the open build are left out.
  • Augment advice rewritten for this week's games. It now always agrees with the Best augment on the page; every other augment says when it is worth taking instead.
  • Every Eternal popup looks the same: the most-picked minors are marked, and who runs it sits at the bottom. Beyond the core is trimmed to the items, with a green +3 wins per 100 games on the ones that win more than a typical item for that hero.
  • Builds say which ranks they show. Plat+ builds need one three-item set with 20 Plat+ games, so most lanes still show all-rank builds under Plat+ for now. The Builds heading now says so.
  • All augments, Eternals and crests are back under Plat+. Plat+ lanes hold only a few hundred games, so some picks were dropping off the list. Every augment shows again, with up to three Eternals and three crests. Picks with too few games to rank are marked few games and can't be Best.

Squad coach

  • Rotations get credit. The coach now knows which lane every kill and tower was in, so a mid who leaves lane to avenge a teammate gets named for it. Staying in your lane while its towers still stand counts as the right play. Once your lane's enemy tower is down, the coach expects you to rotate.
  • No more "never joined" for fights that weren't one fight. Two picks on opposite sides of the map a few seconds apart are now read as two plays, so nobody gets blamed for missing both.
  • Reviews lead with what decided the game. The headline names the cause, then a short ranked list of what lost it (or won it), then one lesson for next game. The times and who-died-where behind each point sit underneath. Newer reviews use it first.
  • Shorter build & matchup breakdowns. Under Evidence & replay details, each section (build decision, lane threats, items, Eternal, augment, crest) is now a short list of points instead of a paragraph.
