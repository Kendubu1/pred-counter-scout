What's new

What changed on Pred Scout, newest first, and what's coming next at the bottom. Short version for players and the 5-stack. The page grows with every update.

Cleaner header, shareable sectionsOct 26

  • BetterPatch notice is a slim bar at the top of the homepage instead of a block in the middle. The "kit · boards" pill is gone; hover the bar to see which patch the numbers come from.
  • BetterMenu regrouped: Meta, Learn, Experimental, then What's new and About. Older patch reviews moved here (bottom of this page).
  • BetterItem swaps on The Climb is a proper two-column table, phones included.
  • NewSection links preview the section. Copy the address while reading a section of The Climb, About or this page and paste it in Discord or iMessage: the preview shows that section, not just the page title.
  • NewThis page.

Plat+ builds, Eternal popups, Most bannedOct 25

Hero pages

  • NewAll ranks / Plat+ switch in the hero header. It changes the whole page together: augment, Eternal, minors, crest and builds. Matchups stay all ranks. Your choice is remembered.
  • BetterBuilds open on the best-winning build with 100+ games, and every tab shows its win rate. Recommendations everywhere follow what's actually winning in ranked; the simulator's kit math is labeled experimental.
  • NewLeveling chart on the Game plan tab: all 18 levels at a glance, readable mid-game.
  • New"How to play" dropdown under the chart: the lane plan, what to watch out for, and a tip per ability.
  • NewTap any Eternal or minor for the whole Eternal: what it gives, why players pick it, who runs it and how often each minor is taken. Learn Eternals shows the same. Eternal lines on hero pages now say what it does for that hero.
  • NewValmont carry gets its own page. It's the one off-lane pick winning clearly (56.5% over 796 ranked games).
  • BetterHero portraits open that hero's page everywhere. A lighter header, tabs that read as tabs, and larger text on phones.
  • FixAsking for a lane a hero has no full build for no longer mixes two lanes' data. The page shows the nearest lane and says so.

Meta board & bans

  • NewMost banned: top bans overall and per lane, by rank. The homepage shows the top 5.
  • New5-stack ban list on the same page: the heroes that beat us most, each one's nemesis on our team, and who stomps us.
  • Better"Show off-meta picks" replaces the games-floor button. Rarely played picks are hidden by default so lane staples lead the board.
  • BetterThe board shows when match data was last refreshed and explains its two numbers. New lane icons.
  • FixThe false "no 1.17.2 data" note: the board covers 1.17 including 1.17.2.

Guides

  • NewItem swaps on The Climb: when Plat+ players actually buy counter items, from 8,331 ranked games. Short version: item 1 for your lane, item 2 for their biggest threat, item 3 back to your build.
  • BetterEvery page gets a sticky section menu, and section links open right at that section.

Coach & 5-stack

  • NewPostgame coaching for two more of our games, each checked by an independent reviewer before publishing.
  • NewStandout builds: games where someone outplayed what that hero usually does in that lane, and why. Every trend is checked against public 1.17.2 win rates.
  • BetterSquad planner is now 5-stack picker, in the menu's Experimental group with Live draft counter.

Coaching and fresh dataOct 24

  • NewPostgame coaching for three of our games.
  • BetterDaily data refresh: hero pages rebuilt from ranked games through Oct 24.

Coming next

  • SoonPatch-only meta board. Today's board pools all of 1.17 (1.17.2 included). From the next data refresh each patch keeps its own numbers, and the board gets a patch filter once there's more than one to pick.
  • SoonItem swap numbers that refresh daily. The Climb's "when the counter swap lands" figures come from one snapshot (Oct 22–24). They'll move into the daily data run so they stay current, and get rechecked on the next patch.
  • SoonAugment playstyle tips. The hero page's "How to play" dropdown will change with your augment: which abilities to lean on and how the combo shifts.
  • ~1 weekRising picks, confirmed or dropped. Argus, Revenant and Drongo offlane and Twinblast mid look like they're climbing. We wait for a week of new games before calling them rising.

  • Later"Does it win?" on counter items. Right now we can show what high-rank players buy, not whether it wins more. That needs a few more weeks of games.
