Patch 1.16 — Aegis of Dawn

Released 2026-08-11 · Scout overview, predictions & measured results · official notes ↗

4 days into the patch · 6,147 ranked matches measured · early read — the meta is still settling, so treat small gaps as noise and check back as the sample grows.
Measured results are in. Every hero card below carries its measured ranked win rate for this patch next to the original call. Of the heroes the notes buffed or nerfed, the stated direction held for 10 of 21.
On each card: ✓ direction held — the win rate moved the way the notes pointed · ✗ moved against the notes — it moved the other way · no mark — the notes did not buff or nerf that hero, so there is no direction to check.

TL;DR — what actually changes how the game plays

A new objective changes every rotation
Shrines grant permanent ability haste, global gold, and true damage to towers — the mini-objective war starts at 2:00 and never stops.
Carries got flattened
Nearly every carry lost 2-4 base physical power with growth compensation: early lane damage is down across the board, scaling is intact. Lane bullies and all-in supports gain relative early power.
Scarlett arrives in the offlane
A melee Energy-resource duelist with a restraining-stun ultimate and three augment paths (mobility, dueling, zone control).
Tower sieging is a plating economy now
Platings never expire and Tier 2 towers get their own — chip damage and Shrine true-damage stacks beat one big dive.
The jungle pays more and punishes more
Camp gold up ~35-40%, regen doubled between clears, and post-20:00 Red/Blue drop a second pickup buff. Junglers snowball harder; chip-clearing is dead.
Four new Eternals and a new minor blessing
Weald (execute projectiles), Rust (stacking shred), Pilow (damage-output debuff) and Satatriel (takedown power at a health cost) join the pool, and Mechadrive — a minor blessing, not an Eternal — replaces the deleted Cauterize. That is five cards marked new in the Eternals section below.
Sustain trimmed again
Potions down another notch, Marrow reworked to missing-health scaling, Immaculate and healing minors shaved — the sustain squeeze from 1.15.3 continues.
Meta read1.16 is a systems patch wearing a balance patch's clothes: Shrines start a permanent mini-objective war at 2:00 (+2 ability haste and 50 global gold per kill, stacking true damage to towers), teleporters open at game start, tower platings never expire, and jungle camps pay ~35-40% more gold. On top of that, a dozen-plus heroes lose 2-4 base physical power with growth compensation — early carry damage drops league-wide, handing relative power to lane bullies, all-in supports, and junglers. The loudest non-change is Rampage: the top winrate hero (55.5% over 16,616 ranked games) receives only a cosmetic-augment hitbox nerf, while the best carry (Sparrow, 51.9%) and the #2 support seat (Phase) take real cuts.
