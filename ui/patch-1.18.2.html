Patch 1.18.2

Releases 2026-11-24 · Scout overview, predictions · official notes ↗

Heads up: these are predictions, not measured results. Every forecast is grounded in the patch's stated numbers and in where each hero stood before 1.18.2. Measured win rates replace the forecasts once enough post-patch ranked games land in the feed.

TL;DR — what actually changes how the game plays

Mid lane gets squishier
Thirteen mages and mid-lane carries lose base health or regen, so poke now sticks and stalling a lane on regen gets harder. The notes expect some carries to lean into mid lane as a result.
Forticus and shield stacking are cut
Forticus costs 3000, shields for 6% max health instead of 8% on a 16-10s cooldown, and Swell grows slower. Phylactery, Azure Core, Soulbearer, Vyzar Carapace and Vortex Stone shields shrink too.
Kaelen comes down
Razorstorm and Frostfall come back slower, Heart of Ice slows 12% instead of 15%, Winter's Pursuit's Frostbite bonus drops to 1.75x, and his growth stats and Last Winter shield are lower.
New items for on-hit and enchanters
Bandit's Toll, Heretic's Blade and Kingsbane give attack-speed heroes new on-hit paths; Shadebinder is a magical bruiser item for Shinbi, Kwang and Aurora; Elysia helps enchanters cast their ultimate more often.
Strong heroes trimmed
Akeron, Ikra, Legion, Narbash, Rampage, Revenant, Riktor, Scarlett, Shinbi, The Fey and TwinBlast all lose something, each aimed at the habit the notes name.
Struggling heroes pulled up
Adele, Boris, GRIM.exe, Sevarog, Skylar and Wukong gain scaling, cooldowns or utility, and Crunch's model shrinks 5%.
Meta read1.18.2 is a durability patch. Thirteen mages lose base health or regen, Forticus and five shield sources shrink, and Kaelen, who sat at 56.8% over 19,912 ranked jungle games, is cut across his kit. Seven items arrive or return for on-hit fighters, bruiser mages and enchanters, with no data yet. Adele, Boris, GRIM.exe, Sevarog, Skylar and Wukong get help.

Ranked comps: what shifts

Expect ranked comps to move away from shield stacking toward damage that sticks: mages get squishier, Forticus and mage shields shrink, and Kaelen comes down. These are reads from the notes, not results.

Mid lane poke matters again
Thirteen mages lose health or regen, so poke now sticks and stalling on regen gets harder. The Fey, at 51.5% over 21,274 midlane games, takes the biggest cut. Expect poke mids and roaming carries to punish slow starts.
Forticus frontlines lose their safety net
Forticus shields for 6% instead of 8% on a longer cooldown, and Vortex Stone, Vyzar Carapace and Terminus shields shrink. Expect fighters who poached Forticus to return to damage items, while true tanks keep it.
Kaelen jungle comes down a step
Kaelen sat at 56.8% over 19,912 ranked jungle games, and Forticus was in 1,629 of his listed core games. Expect him to stay a common pick, with buffed Boris, Sevarog and Wukong as other jungle options.
On-hit carries get new tools
Kingsbane, Bandit's Toll and Heretic's Blade add on-hit paths, and Onixian Quiver's Multishot gets its lost damage back. TwinBlast's Vaporize is cut ahead of them; expect attack-speed carries and GRIM.exe to test them first.
Enchanters pick up Elysia
Elysia gives 25 ultimate haste and faster basic cooldowns after an ultimate; the notes name Zinx, Muriel and Maco. Hexbound Bracers drops from 15 to 10 haste, so expect enchanter supports to move toward Elysia.
