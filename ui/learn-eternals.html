Learn Eternals

An Eternal is a blessing you lock in before the match starts. There are 16 of them, and the choice can look like a wall of text. The short version: each one rewards a different way of playing. Pick the one that matches how you already fight, then use its two minor slots to lean further in.

How the picks work

Three choices, not one

You get the Eternal's Major blessing, which is always on, plus one minor from each of two slots (three options in each).

Chosen before the match

Set in the draft or loadout screen. The blessings come online at level 10.

Saved per hero and role

Your pick sticks to that hero in that role, and you can change it any time.

Chosen by kit fit, not win rate

Per-Eternal win rates are not published to us, so everything here is about how an effect matches a kit — never a field result.

Find yours by playstyle

Tap a playstyle to bring the Eternals that suit it to the top. Nothing is hidden, and an Eternal can suit more than one.
