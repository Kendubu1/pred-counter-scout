Patch 1.16.4

Released 2026-09-01 · Scout overview, predictions · official notes ↗

Heads up: these are predictions, not measured results. The engine's numeric base is still the pre-1.16.4 data, so the sim reads below are the current meta — every forecast is grounded in the patch's stated numbers. Measured win rates replace the forecasts once enough post-patch ranked games land in the feed.

TL;DR — what actually changes how the game plays

Rampage finally gets addressed
The top-winrate hero takes three real nerfs: Tiny Titan no longer removes the combat penalty, armor growth 4.8 -> 4.5, and Behemoth movement speed 5% -> 3%.
Assassins pay the bill
Kallari loses health growth, movement speed, Death Mark damage and three scalings at once. Khaimera's Invigorate stops double-healing off Rage stacks. The air-control cut steers their escapes less, too.
Adele's damage moves out of her team and into her hands
Ally true damage from Crown of Thorns drops 6% -> 5% and her root shortens, while Royal Flourish damage jumps up to 20 points per rank and Imperial Lash scaling climbs 50% -> 80%. Less aura, more spellwork.
The offlane bruisers get fed
Terra's Highland Hurl hits harder and slows 40%, her ult tenacity jumps to 50/100/150. Narbash, Riktor, Steel and Greystone all pick up base-stat or scaling buffs alongside her.
Utility supports lose their speed
Maco's ally movement-speed gift loses a third at max rank (18% -> 12%) and his self buffs now drop on taking hero damage. Phase's Telekinetic Link gives 1% less power at every rank. Renna's shield scaling and ult both come down.
Air control drops league-wide
Mid-air steering falls 90% -> 75% for most of the roster, and the dedicated fliers (Kallari, Eden, Mourn) are trimmed on top. Jump-spam weaving is deliberately weaker.
Eternals tuning wave, no new faces
Weald's missiles need more attack speed and wait longer between heroes, Vesh's Lich restructures to level breakpoints, Krix's minors get broadly kinder, and Satariel now bites 6.5-7.5% health per stack.
Meta readA balance-only patch that trims the top and lifts the floor. Mid-air steering drops from 90% to 75% league-wide, so jumps and dashes commit to their arc, and the camera now tracks aim with no lag. The 1.16 winners (Rampage, Skylar, Greystone, Khaimera) all take real cuts, Kallari's kit is trimmed in five places, and the strugglers (Terra, Ikra, Scarlett, Neon) get targeted lifts. Expect the ranked spread to compress and dive-heavy play to get more honest.
